### ImmerZone's format (the 35.9M Subway Surfers video)
- **3:19 long, 3 levels, each a different world:** L1 City Subway (daylight) → L2 Underground (dark graffiti tunnel) → L3 Lava Zone (on top of trains over lava).
- **A level intro card before each level:** big "Level 1" plus white silhouette figures of the moves, e.g. `RUN + JUMP + DUCK`, on black. The viewer learns the moves first.
- **Moves:** run in place, jump, duck, side-step dodge left/right, **reach up to grab coins**, high knees in L3. Intensity ramps up ("doesn't start at high intensity, starts low" — a top comment).
- **Look:** very polished, stylized cinematic 3D (soft lighting, painterly buildings, Subway Surfers-like trains). A clear step above typical Three.js/Unity copies.
- **Description template:** a story hook ("ATTENTION SURFERS! The grumpy inspector and his dog are on your tail!"), then a "HOW TO PLAY — THE 3 LEVELS" breakdown, then "perfect for 🏠 home cardio / 🏫 PE class & brain breaks", then ~20 hashtags.
- **Translated titles/descriptions:** the same video shows a Spanish title in Spanish search, via YouTube's multi-language metadata.
- **Audience** (from the comments): adults doing cardio ("I CAN'T BREATHE", "can't run 3 minutes") plus kids and teachers. Worldwide: Portuguese, Arabic and Spanish comments.

### Interactive Studio's Demon Slayer video (857K)
- 9 minutes, big on-screen text cues ("TOTAL CONCENTRATION!", "LEVEL 1 BOSS", "FINAL BLOW!"), a boss health bar, a voice counting "one, two…", and anime characters.

## 7. Format rules for OUR videos (apply to every game)

These change a few defaults in `GAME_BUILDER_GUIDE.md`. The winners are **not** fully text-free:

1. **Length 8–10 min** (main series); optional 3–4 min "classroom short" cut.
2. **3–5 levels**, each a clearly different world/time-of-day, with difficulty ramping up. **Start easy.**
3. **Level intro card (3–4 s):** big "LEVEL 1" plus white silhouette icons of the moves (RUN + JUMP + DUCK), no sentences. Teaches the moves.
4. **Move-cue icons before each obstacle:** a big clean icon or silhouette (⬆ JUMP, ⬇ DUCK, ⬅ ➡, 👊 PUNCH, ✋ GRAB) about 1 s before, plus the user's voice-cue MP3s. Short words (JUMP!) are fine. Keep the rest of the screen clean.
5. **Extra body moves beyond the runner controls** (this is what makes it a workout):
   - run in place (constant)
   - reach up to grab coins/stars
   - high knees in the lava/boost zone
   - squat (duck)
   - punch combos at the smash wall ×3
   - push (cart)
   - arm circles / "power move" moments
   - 5–10 s **rest/breathe** beats between levels
6. **Boss / chase finale** with a health bar (each punch reduces it). Countdown voice "3-2-1". Victory screen, confetti, "SUBSCRIBE for Level 2".
7. **Story hook in the first 5 s** ("ATTENTION ELVES! The Grinch stole Christmas!"), a villain chasing behind you, stakes.
8. **Visual bar:** stylized cinematic 3D, bright, clean, 4K 60fps export. This is our edge vs the thousands of low-quality copies. (GAME_BUILDER_GUIDE Section 3.3 checklist.)
9. **Series thinking:** number every video (#1, #2 …), make "Part 2 / Level 2" sequels of winners, and use crossovers (IP × IP, e.g. "Subway Surfers × Pokémon", "Sonic × Mario" 2.9M).

## 10. Risks
- **IP / copyright:** these are unofficial fan-made videos using brand characters. The official Subway Surfers channel (10.1M subs) now posts its own warm-ups. Keep characters as original low-poly fan-art (no ripped models, logos or music), and put "fan-made, not affiliated" in the description. Claims and strikes are possible; monetization risk is the owner's call.
- **Made for Kids:** classroom/kids content may need the "Made for Kids" setting, which limits comments and personalized ads (lower RPM).
- **Trend decay:** Roblox Keyboard Escape and K-Pop Demon Hunters peak fast. Ride them only if you can publish within 1–2 weeks.
