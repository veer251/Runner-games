CONCEPT
-------
A first-person (FPP) runner set in the Demon Slayer universe where the player runs through iconic locations
from the Wisteria Forest to the Infinity Castle. The player wields a katana, performs breathing techniques,
rescues Nezuko, and fights an Upper Moon boss. The emotional hook is the bonds between the Demon Slayer
Corps — the same love for Tanjiro and his friends that millions of fans feel watching the show.

- The katana slash mechanic with a Water Breathing trail makes every viewer want to try it

PLAYER (FPP VIEW)
-----------------
The player sees Tanjiro's black-checkered haori sleeve and a katana blade in the right hand. The blade gleams
with a Water Breathing blue shimmer when a slash is performed. No brim or hat is visible from the FPP camera.
During the slash, a flowing water-trail arc sweeps across the screen in translucent blue.

LEVELS
------

LEVEL 1 — WISTERIA FOREST (moonlit night, serene and eerie)
  Scenery: Falling wisteria petals (particles), ancient stone lanterns lining a dirt path, gnarled
           moonlit trees, distant mountains under a dark blue sky, low mist at ground level.
  Obstacles:
    JUMP: Fallen logs and jutting tree roots crossing the path
    DUCK: Shimenawa rope stretched between two trees at head height
    DODGE: Demons lurching out of the tree line into the player's lane
  SET PIECE — CART: The supply cart carrying Nezuko's glowing pink box — hold Shift to push it forward; shout "PROTECT NEZUKO!"
  SET PIECE — WALL: Three stone-and-wood shrine gates (torii) stacked as a barricade, smashed x3 with Shift

LEVEL 2 — INFINITY CASTLE (warm candlelight, otherworldly and red-tinted)
  Scenery: Floating tatami rooms at impossible angles, red torii gates as pillars, paper shoji screens
           sliding open and shut, silk banners in crimson and black, a full moon visible through
           a gap in the twisting architecture.
  Obstacles:
    JUMP: Raised tatami platform edges
    DUCK: Low noren curtain beams hanging across the corridor
    DODGE: Sliding shoji panels and demons stepping from behind them
  POSE WALLS (5):
    1. WATER BREATHING (warrior lunge) — Tanjiro's Form 3 stance from the show
    2. WIND STANCE (star spread) — Inosuke's wild dual-blade open stance
    3. THUNDER (crane one-leg) — Zenitsu's lightning-leg pose
    4. TOTAL CONCENTRATION (tpose arms wide) — the breathing technique meditation pose
    5. SLAYER'S SALUTE (xarms over chest) — the Demon Slayer Corps salute
  SET PIECE — WALL: A solid shoji-and-wood barrier x3

LEVEL 3 — NIGHT TRAIN ROOFTOP (Mugen Train, speeding night, FASTEST) ★ SIGNATURE MOVE: KATANA SLASH
  Scenery: The black-and-red Mugen Train speeding through a mountain tunnel and out into a starry night;
           telegraph poles flashing past, sparks from the rails below, the train's chimney blasting steam.
  Obstacles:
    JUMP: Flesh ridges rising across the roof
    DUCK: Steel bridge crossbeams passing low overhead
    DODGE: Engine vents and protruding metal rivets
  SIGNATURE: A flesh tentacle rises in the player's lane; Shift = the katana slashes with a Water Breathing
             blue-white arc trail. The tentacle bursts into pink particles and the screen flashes.
             Big text: "AIM FOR THE NAPE!" / "HINOKAMI!"

LEVEL 4 — BOSS: UPPER MOON (floating tatami arena, snowflake aura)
  Boss appearance: Tall and pale with long silver-white hair, dressed in a white haori with a snowflake
                   pattern and a crescent moon kanji on his eye. Surrounded by drifting ice crystals.
  Attack patterns:
    A (LEFT): Blue orb flies from the left — press A to slash it
    D (RIGHT): Blue orb flies from the right — press D to slash it
    SHIFT/X: Pink X-shaped cross-slash — press Shift to counter-slash
    S (DUCK): Red shockwave ring sweeps along the ground — duck to avoid it
  ROUND 3 SPECIAL (R3): Boss spawns two orbs simultaneously; screen edge glows purple; "FINAL ROUND!" appears
  FINAL BLOW: The player and Tanjiro combine attacks — a dual Water Breathing + Sun Breathing spiral beam.
              The boss shatters into snowflakes and blue light. "FINAL BLOW!" in red-gold kanji style.
  CELEBRATION: The sun rises (dawn sky preset) over the Infinity Castle. Tanjiro, Nezuko (she leaps out of
               the box), Zenitsu and Inosuke gather. Sparkle confetti falls. Text: "DEDICATE YOUR HEARTS!"
